Privacy Policy for OmniCalc PRO

Your privacy is important to us. This document explains how OmniCalc PRO handles user information, device data, and third-party advertising services.

App: OmniCalc PRO Developer: Aman Beer Package: com.amankhowalhr.omnicalcpro Last Updated: October 2026
🔒 Privacy Summary: OmniCalc PRO works primarily offline. All calculation inputs, parameters, and history records remain stored strictly on your local device. We do not operate external database servers or collect personal user accounts. Third-party advertising (Google AdMob) may collect standard advertising identifiers to serve non-intrusive ads.

1. Introduction

OmniCalc PRO is developed by Aman Beer ("we", "our", or "us") as a comprehensive global utility application for Android. The application encompasses an extensive suite of calculation modules, including:

  • Core Mathematics & Utilities: Standard, Scientific, Unit Converter, Currency Converter, Percentage, Tip, Date/Age, Time Zone, and Random Number Generation.
  • Health & Body: BMI (Body Mass Index), BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate), Body Fat Percentage, Daily Calorie Requirements, Ideal Body Weight, Water/Hydration, Target Heart Rate, and Pregnancy Due Date Calculator.
  • Finance & Loans: Loan/EMI Calculator with Amortization Schedules, Compound Interest, Investment Returns, and Inflation.
  • Income & Commerce: Income Tax Estimators, Salary Breakdown, Shopping Discount, Sales Tax / GST, and Profit Margin.

By installing or using OmniCalc PRO, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Local Data Storage & User Privacy

We strongly believe that your personal calculations and financial or health inputs belong entirely to you.

  • Offline-First Architecture: The calculation engines, historical records, and user preferences run locally on your Android device without requiring an active internet connection or account creation.
  • Local Calculation History: When you save calculation records (such as BMI records, Loan estimates, or mathematical equations), they are saved securely in your device's private application storage using SQLite / Room database.
  • No Cloud Uploads: We do not operate proprietary web servers, databases, or cloud accounts that receive, transmit, or store your calculation entries, body measurements, income figures, or transaction records.
  • User Control: You can clear your calculation history at any time directly through the app interface or by clearing the application storage via Android Settings.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

OmniCalc PRO is supported by advertising to allow free distribution and continuous maintenance. To serve ads, the application integrates Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC.

While OmniCalc PRO does not directly collect any personal identity information, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect certain device telemetry and analytics data to deliver advertisements, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance.

Data Collected by Google AdMob May Include:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID), Android ID, device manufacturer, model, and operating system version.
  • IP Address: Used primarily for regional ad localization, coarse geographic routing, and fraud detection.
  • Ad Interaction Data: Information regarding ad impressions, clicks, user interactions, and performance diagnostics.
  • Crash Data & Diagnostics: Anonymous technical logs to ensure ad rendering stability.

For detailed information on how Google processes, secures, and protects your information, please review the official documentation:

  • Google Privacy & Terms
  • Google AdMob Data Collection & Privacy Policy
  • Google Ad Personalization Controls

Opting Out of Personalized Advertising:

You can control or opt out of personalized interest-based advertising on your Android device at any time by going to:

Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

4. Android Permissions

OmniCalc PRO requires minimal device permissions strictly necessary for its advertised operations:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required exclusively by the Google AdMob SDK to load advertisement creatives and check connectivity for online currency exchange rate references. The app remains fully operational for calculations when offline.

OmniCalc PRO does NOT request access to contacts, call logs, SMS, camera, microphone, microphone audio, precise GPS location, or external files.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

OmniCalc PRO is a general utility tool not targeted toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children. If we discover that a child has provided personal information to a third-party SDK through our app, we will take immediate steps to address and remediate it.

6. Data Security

We value your trust in using OmniCalc PRO. Because all your calculation logs and parameters remain strictly within your device's private sandboxed app directory, your data is shielded by Android's core operating system application sandboxing security model.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates, regulatory requirements, or third-party service changes. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any modifications.
